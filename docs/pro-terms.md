## The short version
Momentum Life is **free and local**. Your notes are plain Markdown files in your own vault and never depend on us. **Pro** is a one-time purchase that unlocks a few extra, cloud-based conveniences. Buying Pro means you accept the terms below, including the risk that the project may one day be discontinued.

## Free vs Pro (freemium)
- **Free, forever:** everything that runs on your own vault — tasks, habits, fitness, nutrition, studies, journal, finances, the file manager, and **text/Markdown Google Drive sync**. These work offline and do not require an account with us or any payment.
- **Pro (one-time unlock):** syncing **binary files** (images, PDFs and other non-text files) through Google Drive. Everything your notes need already works for free; Pro just adds binaries on top.
